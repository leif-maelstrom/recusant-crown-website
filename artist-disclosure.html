Artist Commission Disclosure

What you should know before working with Recusant Crown

Who We Are

I am a single creator, Magi Grimm. This project began in 2019 as a series of pen drawings on pallet cards at work. I have been making creative work since 2012, when I started Gifted, a webcomic that has since gone down. I still work in industry to pay the bills while I build projects like this one. I say this not as a brag or self-deprecation, but so you know I am in the trenches of small-time creation alongside you. I have been doing this almost my entire adult life.

About This Document

This is not a contract. This document exists so you know exactly what this project is, how your work will be used, and what you're agreeing to before we invest time in a formal agreement. Read it fully. If anything here doesn't work for you, no hard feelings — better we both know now.

The Project

Recusant Crown is a copyleft multimedia project: a trading card game built around short stories, illustrations, and original faerie tales. The core mechanic is Dreaming — a form of planeswalking through a multiverse made of dream. The project's central tension is light versus darkness, but neither maps cleanly onto good versus evil.

The project is deliberately copyleft. Every asset, including commissioned art, is released under copyleft licensing. This is a political choice: we want to force corporate media to lose creative control if they choose to use our material. Your work cannot be locked away by a corporation. It belongs to the commons, with your credit attached.

AI Disclosure

This project uses AI tools in its production workflow. Some assets on the site and in the game are AI-assisted or AI-generated, then refined by human hands. We are transparent about this because we believe artists deserve to know exactly what they're contributing to.

Your commissioned pieces are your original human work. We are not asking you to use AI, and we will not run your finished pieces through AI tools. The AI disclosure exists so you understand the project's broader workflow — your art will sit alongside AI-assisted assets on the website and in the game. If that's a problem for you, this is the moment to walk away.

The Commission

We are currently commissioning five (5) outside artist pieces as spoiler cards for the Recusant Crown website. These cards are the public face of the project's collaborative nature. They prove Recusant Crown is built by a community of creators, not a solo operation.

Rate: $50 per accepted piece.

What "accepted" means: You will submit your piece. If it meets the criteria below, it is accepted and you are paid. If it does not meet the criteria, you will receive feedback and one (1) revision pass. If the revision still does not meet the criteria, the piece is declined and no payment is made. We cannot afford to pay for pieces we cannot use. This is stated plainly so there is no surprise later.

Creative Freedom

You choose the card you want to illustrate. We will provide a list of card names that currently lack art. These are the cards we are accepting submissions for.

You have complete freedom over medium, style, and interpretation. The game's themes are dreams, stories, nightmares, and art itself. The stronger your personal style comes through, the better your piece will be. We are not hiring you to imitate an existing look — we are hiring you because your voice is distinct.

Pieces are judged on three criteria:

Distinctiveness — Does this piece look like it could only come from you?

Finish — Is the piece polished and complete?

Creativity — How compelling is your interpretation of the card name and type?

You will receive a short write-up describing the universe these cards inhabit, plus character references if your chosen card is tied to a series character.

What You Get

  • $50 per accepted piece. Paid on acceptance.
  • Spoiler card placement on the website. Your work will be publicly visible as one of the first outside artist pieces in the project.
  • Brand language credit. If the project grows, your style becomes part of Recusant Crown's visual identity.
  • Future commission potential. Strong work on this batch could lead to commissions for future faerie tale and related projects as the Kickstarter develops.
  • Copyleft credit. Your name stays attached to your work. Always.

Timeline

We need the five pieces within two (2) months of commission confirmation. The art will be used for advertising as we build toward a Kickstarter launch in the next one to two years.

The Honest Part

$50 per piece is a low rate. We know this. We are a small operation in the early stages of a launch window, and we cannot pay more right now. What we are offering instead:

  • Early entry into a project we believe will grow
  • Public placement as a founding collaborator
  • Creative freedom most commercial commissions never allow
  • A copyleft ethos that treats your work as part of a commons, not corporate property

If that trade doesn't work for you, we understand completely. If it does, we would be honored to have your voice in this project.

This document is a disclosure, not a contract. Formal agreements will be made per piece upon acceptance of the commission terms.
