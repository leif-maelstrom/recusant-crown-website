Card Game

Growing flexibility over raw numbers

Rules

The core idea is simple: power comes from options, not just bigger numbers. Collecting cards increases your flexibility — the more pieces you have, the more ways you can respond.

Full rules are still being refined. Here's the current high-level shape:

  • A lane based anime combat game. Deploy minions, push lanes, gather energy and strike with powerful spells and technics!
  • Gather Clarity from playing minions of your preferred faction.
  • Clash in a massive burst of violence.
  • Use the resources gathered from these clashes to pull off stylish combos, flashy spells, and might summons.

Detailed rules will appear here as they stabilize.
