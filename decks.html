Ready-to-play starter decks, free to save, print, and play at home

Starter Decks

Fixed 60-card decks, built to be fun first: each one can win, has a few splashy spells, and hides combos for you to find. Each Starter is a matched pair: Dream vs Dark, and Resolve vs Light. Resolve and Light hit about a third harder, so mixing pairs (Resolve vs Dark, say) leans toward the bigger deck. Pick one to see its cards.

Deck files stay on your computer; opening one only reads it in your browser.

Cards print at true size (2.5 × 3.5 in), nine to a page. In the print dialog, set scale to 100% (or “Actual size”). Choose “Save as PDF” as the printer to get a PDF.
