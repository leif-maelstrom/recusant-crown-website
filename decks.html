Starter Decks

Fixed 60-card decks, built to be fun first: each one can win, has a few splashy spells, and hides combos for you to find. Each Starter is a matched pair: Dream vs Dark, and Resolve vs Light. Resolve and Light win by fighting; Dream and Dark have smaller creatures and win by firing their engines, so mixing pairs (Resolve vs Dark, say) leans toward the bigger creatures. Pick one to see its cards, or edit a copy to make it your own.

Deck files stay on your computer; opening one only reads it in your browser.

Cards print at true size (2.5 × 3.5 in), nine to a page. In the print dialog, set scale to 100% (or “Actual size”). Choose “Save as PDF” as the printer to get a PDF.

    Playing in Tabletop Simulator

    Any deck on this page, starter or your own, can go straight into Tabletop Simulator.

    1. Press Export for Tabletop Simulator on the deck. You get a small .json file.
    2. Put it in your Saved Objects folder: Documents/My Games/Tabletop Simulator/Saves/Saved Objects (on a Mac, ~/Library/Tabletop Simulator/Saves/Saved Objects).
    3. In a game, open Objects › Saved Objects and click the deck to spawn it. Right-click it and shuffle.

    The card images load from this site the first time, then Tabletop Simulator keeps its own copy. When the cards change, export the deck again to get the new versions.
